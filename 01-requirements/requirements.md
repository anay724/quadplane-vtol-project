REQ-## | Requirement | Method of Verification

REQ-01 | The drone shall hover for at least 2 minutes at MTOW. | Inspection
REQ-02 | The drone shall cruise for at least 15 minutes at a speed of ___ m/s. | Test
REQ-03 | The drone shall have a MTOW (maximum take off weight) of no more than 2.27 kg (5 lb) at MTOW. | Test
REQ-04 | The drone shall have a T/W (Thrust-to-Weight) ratio of at least 2.0. | Analysis
REQ-05 | The drone shall be flown only in FAA approved sites and following TAMU UAS policy if flown on/near campus (https://ehs.tamu.edu/info-for/unmanned-aerial-systems.html).
